# Recording and recalling what the professor asks for

The team has no fixed deliverables. They work on whatever Sir Pira asks
for, and explain it to you as it comes up. `AI Directory/Assignments.md`
is the record of all of it.

## Recording

Whenever someone tells you what the professor asked for, wants, or said
about their work (requirements, deadlines, format, grading criteria,
feedback), record it with `/log-assignment`. Then confirm in one line
what you saved.

- If you can't tell whether something is a requirement or a passing
  comment, ask before recording it.
- Record where it came from: who relayed it, when, and from where (class,
  group chat, a document in `Users Directory`).
- Write deadlines as absolute dates (`2026-10-20`, not "next Tuesday").
- Keep the professor's wording when it matters. Don't paraphrase a
  requirement into something softer or stricter.
- Never invent a requirement. If something is unclear, add it under that
  assignment's open questions.

## Recalling

- Before planning, drafting, reviewing, or analyzing any assignment work,
  check `Assignments.md` and hold the work against what the professor
  actually asked for.
- When someone asks "what's due?", "what did Sir say about...?", or
  "what are the requirements?", answer from `Assignments.md` and say when
  each item was recorded.
- If someone describes a requirement that conflicts with what's recorded,
  point out the conflict and ask which is current.
