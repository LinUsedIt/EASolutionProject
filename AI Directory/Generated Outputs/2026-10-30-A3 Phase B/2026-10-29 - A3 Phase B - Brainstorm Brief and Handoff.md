## 4. Baseline (current state)

### 4.1 Mentor-Mentee Pairing (baseline)
1. The **Registrar** releases the list of enrolled students (name, email,
   course, year level). The list goes **either** Registrar → OSM →
   coordinator, **or** the coordinator requests it from the Registrar
   directly. (Aldrin; Lactao; Olmos [26:24])
2. The list is sorted by school and goes to each school's coordinator. The
   male and female coordinators split it by sex. (Aldrin; Olmos)
3. Returning students get a Google Form asking whether they want to keep
   last year's mentor. Only about 10-20% answer. (Lactao)
4. The coordinator rebuilds last year's pairings by hand because the final
   list was not saved, removing graduates using the graduation program.
   (Lactao; Olmos [24:31])
5. The coordinator emails mentors to ask whether they can take more
   mentees (capacity check). (Lactao)
6. The coordinator pairs students with available mentors from the same
   school. If no mentor has room, the coordinator takes the student as
   their own mentee or gives the student to a mentor from another school.
   (Aldrin; Olmos; Lactao)
7. The process ends with the final list of mentee-mentor pairs. The
   coordinator emails each mentor their mentee list, and OSM emails each
   student their assigned mentor. (Aldrin; Lactao)

### 4.2 Mentor Change (baseline)
1. The student emails OSM or the coordinator to request a change.
   (Olmos [31:03]; Em's Notes 2)
2. OSM relays the request to the school coordinator by email.
3. The coordinator asks whether the student has a preferred mentor. If
   not, the coordinator gives the student a list. (Lactao)
4. The coordinator emails the candidate mentor. If the mentor declines,
   the coordinator tries another. (Lactao)
5. The coordinator sends the updated mentee list with OSM (Ms. Nina) in
   CC and informs the student. (Lactao)
6. Typical turnaround is about 2 days. (Olmos [34:08])
7. Mentors can also start a change, for example asking OSM to reassign
   mentees who never reply. (Olmos [42:13])
8. Em's Notes 2 calls the volume of mentor-change requests the unit's
   biggest problem. It is driven mainly by freshmen (Olmos [43:58]), and
   there is no process to prevent changes.

### 4.3 Other baseline facts
- **Session logging:** the student submits a Google Form. It goes to OSM,
  then to the mentor, who confirms it or requests a correction. Confirming
  triggers a required "How was the mentoring?" question. (Olmos [39:57])
- **The two Google Sheets are NOT connected** (Aldrin, 2026-10-29). Students
  fill the session-log sheet through the Form, and the mentoring
  coordinators handle the pairing list. This **overrides Em's Notes 2**
  ("IT Office already linked the 2 Google Sheets"). Em should re-check it.
- **Mentor approval chain:** the supervisor recommends, then OpCom, then OSM
  compiles the list, then ManCom approves. A confidential scorecard is used.
  (Lactao; Em's Notes 2)
- **Mentor load and shortage:** loads range from 2 to 50 mentees per
  mentor, set by each mentor's willingness. Mentors are short in some
  schools, for example the School of Management. (Lactao; Olmos [27:39])
- **Other actors:** Guidance Office (referrals), OpCom, HR (MDP/IDP),
  FreshStart. (Lactao; Olmos; Em's Notes 2)
- **Left out on purpose:** the old Dragon's Lair mentoring platform
  (Aldrin, 2026-10-29).

## 5. Target state (team's proposal, for sponsor approval)

- **Business-level target:** all mentoring workflows (pairing, mentor
  change, mentee contact, session logging, monitoring) run as **one
  centralized, end-to-end workflow** with **one shared source of truth**
  for pairings, replacing email chains and per-coordinator spreadsheets.
  (Aldrin, 2026-10-29)
- **Handed to Phase C (Clark) as a requirement:** a **single
  program/website/application** that supports these workflows. Phase B
  states why it is needed, and Phase C chooses it.
- **Built-in safeguards:** a single Registrar feed into the register, and a
  named data owner at OSM who maintains it every term.
- **Later transition step, not the target:** automated or assisted
  mentor-student matching. The unit wants it (Em's Notes 2), but the
  coordinators keep the pairing decision.
- **Target pairing:** Registrar → OSM (one route) → central register,
  sorted by school and sex → returning students confirm their mentor in the
  system → mentors declare their capacity → the coordinator pairs within
  the school using the register → overflow is recorded in the register →
  automatic notifications.
- **Target mentor change:** the student submits a request in the system →
  one tracked queue visible to OSM → the coordinator assigns a mentor with
  capacity → the mentor accepts → the register updates automatically and
  everyone is notified.
