# Updated Study Guides

Updated study guides for every chapter (1-22) of *Mastering the TOGAF
Standard* (Eric Jager, 2025), made on 2026-10-23. They follow the
`/make-study-guide` template: Overview, Detailed Section Breakdown, Key
Terms & Definitions, Figures & Tables Referenced, and Self-Check Questions,
with the book's page numbers throughout. The older guides for chapters
1-12 are still in `Course Files/Study Guides/`.
