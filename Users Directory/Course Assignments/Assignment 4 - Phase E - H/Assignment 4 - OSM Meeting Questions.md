# Assignment 4: What We Need From the OSM (and Sample Questions)

Prepared 2026-11-10 by Laufey. For the team to use in the next meeting with
the Mentoring Unit (Ms. Nina, Ms. Kelai) and, if possible, the sponsor and
the IT Office. This is a draft; edit freely.

## 1. The idea in plain words

Phases A to D answered: **"What does the unit do, what is wrong, and what
should it look like?"** We learned that from the interviews.

Phases E to H answer a different set of questions: **"How do we actually get
there, who is in charge, how do we know it's working, and what happens when
things change?"** Those are decisions and commitments, and only the unit can
make them. We can't guess them from past interviews, and we shouldn't invent
them.

| Phase | The plain question it answers | What we need from the OSM |
|---|---|---|
| **E: Opportunities and Solutions** | What do we do first, second, third? | Which improvements matter most, what must happen before what, how much time and how many people each needs |
| **F: Migration Planning** | How do we move safely, and who watches over it? | Who owns the change, who approves it, what money or support exists, and when the unit can handle changes |
| **G: Implementation Governance** | How do we check that what gets built matches the plan? | What "working" looks like, who would check, and which measures they trust |
| **H: Architecture Change Management** | What happens when something changes later? | How the unit handles a change today: who decides, who is told, how |
| **Requirements Management** | Are our requirements still true? | A quick check that the requirements we wrote (from the interviews) still hold |

## 2. What we already know (so we don't ask again)

- The unit has 2 staff, about 1,500 to 1,600 students, about 180 mentors.
- The catalyst is 3 sessions per semester, and two missed semesters in a row
  affects enrollment.
- Pain points: mentor-change email chains, reliance on schools and the
  Registrar for pairings, doubt about students logging sessions.
- Each school's coordinator decides pairings. The session-log sheet and the
  coordinators' pairing lists are not connected.
- We drafted four work packages in the Roadmap (a single pairing register,
  a central mentor-change workflow, session logging, and automated matching
  later).

Mark who is best placed: **N/K** (Ms. Nina or Ms. Kelai), **S** (sponsor),

## 4. How to run the meeting

- **Keep it short:** 30 to 45 minutes. Priorities first (questions 1 to 4),
  then ownership and approval (7 to 9), then success and change.
- **Show, don't lecture:** bring the Roadmap work packages and Phase B target
  workflow diagram on one page and ask them to react.
- **Record answers verbatim** where possible, and ask permission to record or
  take notes. Save notes in this folder as `OSM Meeting - Notes - YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- **Don't invent answers.** Anything not answered stays `[TO CONFIRM]` in the
  documents.
- **Tell us if the Registrar or a school's coordinator must be asked.** The
  unit may not have authority to answer for them.
